Who We Are

Rooted in Faith, Driven by Hope, Devoted to Community Renewal.

Established in 1997 in the mountainous slopes of Bududa District, Trauma Healing Childcare and Development Association (THECCODA) has stood as a beacon of refuge, restorative counseling, and social uplift for 29 years.

Our headquarters at Makalama Village, Bukibino Sub-County, Manjiya County serves as the epicenter of holistic assistance for orphaned children, elderly persons, people living with disabilities, and families suffering from grief, displacement, and economic hardship.

As an accredited National and Local NGO, we integrate specialized clinical psychosocial therapy with community-based faith and hope guidance, ensuring that every wounded soul discovers healing, dignity, and a sustainable pathway forward.

National & Local Mandate Serving grassroots villages in Bududa alongside national coordination networks across Uganda.
Faith & Hope Foundation Grounded in unconditional love, spiritual resilience, and dignified community empowerment.

Purpose & Principles

Our Vision, Mission & Core Values

The foundational ethos directing our service to vulnerable persons, child sanctuary havens, and community development across Uganda.

Vision ✨

A world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

Ensuring equitable access to trauma healing, safe haven care, nutrition, and restorative social welfare so that every vulnerable person can lead a fulfilled and dignified life.

Mission

Bring people together to build hope, peace and community.

Uniting families, caregivers, therapists, and partners through faith-guided compassion and sustainable community programs that break the cycle of trauma and despair.

Core Values

1 ❤️

Compassion and dignity

Serving every hurting heart and vulnerable individual with boundless love, deep empathy, and profound respect.

2 🌱

3 ⚖️

Building trusted coalitions with local residents, health leaders, institutions, and global supporters for shared impact.

Our Core Pillars

What We Do: Holistic Community Restoration

Guided by our slogan “Building a community driven with faith and hope”, THECCODA concentrates on four interconnected humanitarian pillars to break cycles of despair.

🤝
Pillar One

Caring for Vulnerable Persons

Dedicated social welfare, direct food rations, home medical visits, warm bedding, and assistive mobility equipment for orphans, destitute widows, neglected elders, and persons with disabilities across Bududa and Eastern Uganda.

  • Nutritional relief and emergency care packages for impoverished households
  • Home-based healthcare and palliative support for bedridden elders
  • Disaster response and shelter restoration for landslide-affected families
Learn More →
🕊️
Pillar Two

Counseling, Guidance & Trauma Healing

Clinical psychosocial counseling, bereavement guidance, child play therapy, and community faith-and-hope circles to help survivors process pain, overcome trauma, and restore mental wellness.

  • Specialized one-on-one therapy for victims of trauma, loss, and violence
  • Faith & Hope Community Circles fostering collective empathy and reconciliation
  • Psychological first aid and life-skills guidance for youth and adolescents
Learn More →
🌱
Pillar Three

Childcare, Protection & Education Support

Transitional safe haven care in Makalama, loving early childhood daycare, full school bursary sponsorships, learning kits, and rigorous child rights defense in partnership with district authorities.

  • Makalama Safe Haven sanctuary beds with 24/7 loving house parent care
  • Comprehensive primary and secondary education fees, uniforms, and textbooks
  • Toll-free 24/7 child crisis protection hotline: +256 800 222 888
Learn More →
🌾
Pillar Four

Community Development & Livelihoods

Economic independence through 84+ Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs), women’s tailoring and baking micro-enterprises, regenerative mountain farming, and gravity-fed clean water points.

  • Start-up seed grants for widowed caregivers and foster mothers
  • Youth vocational training in carpentry, tailoring, and electrical trades
  • Protected hillside spring water projects serving over 14 mountain villages
