Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: March 2026 • Makalama Village, Bududa District

Trauma Healing Childcare and Development Association (THECCODA) utilizes privacy-respecting browser local storage solely to support essential website features and CMS database operations.

1. Essential Local Storage

We store donation calculation states, copy-to-clipboard transaction references, event RSVP passes, and staff CMS demonstration state. These are held locally on your device.

2. Zero Commercial Advertising Trackers

THECCODA does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, data-broker tracking scripts, or commercial cross-site monitors.
