Careers at THECCODA

We are looking for dedicated professionals who share our mission to bring people together to build hope, peace and community, advancing our vision of a world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

Organizational Culture

What We Value in Every Team Member

At THECCODA, every staff member and field caseworker is an ambassador of hope. Candidates must demonstrate deep personal alignment with our 5 core values:

1. Compassion and dignity Treating every vulnerable child and adult with unconditional kindness, honor, and empathy.
2. Resilience Steadfast endurance in difficult mountain conditions, nurturing persistent optimism.
3. Integrity & equity Absolute honesty, fairness, and zero tolerance for discrimination or nepotism.
4. Accountability Clear stewardship of organizational resources, strict safeguarding, and dependable execution.
5. Collaboration Listening actively, valuing community wisdom, and working in unity with teammates.

Counseling & Guidance

Senior Community Counselor & Trauma Healing Lead

Full-time
Location: Makalama Headquarters, Bududa District • Deadline: April 15, 2026

Leading community trauma healing circles, supervising individual guidance sessions, and mentoring grassroots volunteer counselors in Bukibino and neighboring sub-counties.

Requirements:
  • Degree or Postgraduate Diploma in Counseling Psychology, Social Work, or Guidance
  • Minimum 3 years of field experience in community psychosocial support or trauma rehabilitation
  • Fluency in Lumasaaba (Lugisu) and English is essential
  • Deep empathy and commitment to faith-and-hope community service
Child Protection & Welfare

Child Welfare & Safeguarding Haven Officer

Full-time
Location: Makalama Haven Center & Field Outreaches • Deadline: April 20, 2026

Managing child safe haven intake, coordinating scholastic bursaries, conducting family tracing, and liaising with Bududa district probation authorities.

Requirements:
  • Degree in Social Work and Social Administration (SWASA) or Child Development
  • Proven track record managing child protection and vulnerable persons cases
  • Clear police background check and child safeguarding certificate
Livelihoods & Development

Community Livelihoods & VSLA Officer

Full-time
Location: Bukibino Sub-County & Manjiya County • Deadline: April 25, 2026

Facilitating savings and credit cooperatives for widows and caregivers of vulnerable children, delivering micro-enterprise business training, and monitoring revolving loan funds.

Requirements:
  • Diploma or Degree in Agribusiness, Microfinance, Economics, or Community Development
  • 2+ years experience facilitating grassroots Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs)
  • Motorcycle riding license and comfort navigating mountain slopes
