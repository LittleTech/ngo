Trauma Healing, Childcare & Community Development

Trauma Healing Childcare and Development Association (THECCODA) is an accredited National and Local NGO in Uganda. For over 29 years, we have served vulnerable persons, providing clinical counseling, compassionate childcare, and sustainable community empowerment from our home in Bududa District.

Our Heritage

Founded in Makalama Village, 1997

In 1997, community elders, faith leaders, and social advocates came together in Makalama village to respond to severe humanitarian distress in the mountain slopes of Bududa District.

Located at Makalama Village, Bukibino Sub-County, Manjiya County in Bududa District, the surrounding terrain on the slopes of Mount Elgon is known for extraordinary natural beauty, but also for devastating natural mudslides, geographic isolation, and severe economic deprivation. Vulnerable orphans, destitute widows, neglected elders, and persons with disabilities were left without safety nets.

What began as a localized community prayer fellowship and relief kitchen grew into THECCODA. We recognized that material aid alone could not heal deep emotional wounds: children and families who had lost their homes and loved ones needed specialized psychosocial trauma counseling, spiritual reassurance, safe shelter, and economic self-reliance.

Today, THECCODA is a registered National and Local NGO operating across Eastern Uganda and maintaining policy linkages in Kampala, having reached over 38,500 vulnerable individuals since 1997.

Our Vision ✨

A world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

We envision an equitable society where systemic barriers are removed, vulnerable children, elderly, and displaced persons receive high-quality counseling, healthcare, and sanctuary, and every human being lives with dignity and opportunity.

Our Mission

Bring people together to build hope, peace and community.

Through collaborative grassroots action, clinical psychosocial therapy, haven protection, and faith-inspired fellowship, we mobilize families, leaders, and partners to heal wounds of trauma and restore lasting community peace.

29 Years of Impact

Our Milestone Journey (1997 – 2026)

From humble beginnings in Makalama village to an accredited National and Local NGO serving thousands across Uganda.

1997

Founding in Makalama Village, Bududa

Community elders and pastors establish the association in Makalama, Bukibino sub-county, providing emergency meals, warm clothing, and prayer counseling for disaster-affected families and orphans.

2002

Establishment of Makalama Safe Haven Sanctuary

Constructed our first permanent residential sanctuary in Makalama, providing 24/7 safe haven beds, pediatric medical triage, and nutritious daily feeding for abandoned and destitute children.

2008

National & Local NGO Registration

Formal statutory registration granted under the Uganda Non-Governmental Organisations Act (Reg. #INBR199708), establishing institutional governance, statutory annual auditing, and formal district partnerships.

2014

Pioneering Clinical Trauma Psychotherapy & Expressive Play

Integrated licensed clinical psychologists into field operations. Launched dedicated child-friendly expressive art therapy rooms and community bereavement guidance circles for landslide survivors.

2020

Caregiver Economic Empowerment (84+ VSLAs)

Scaled our Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) across Bukibino and neighboring parishes, equipping over 1,500 widowed mothers and caregivers with micro-enterprise seed capital.

2026

Celebrating 29 Years • Upgraded Global Giving Portal

Commemorating 29 years of uninterrupted community service with 38,500+ lives restored. Launched digital mobile money (MTN & Airtel) and international bank wire giving channels to accelerate outreach.
