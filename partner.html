Strategic Alliances • Local & International

Partner With THECCODA

We collaborate with bilateral aid programs, philanthropic foundations, church alliances, and international NGOs in service of our mission to bring people together to build hope, peace and community and our vision of a world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

Partnership Grounded in Our 5 Core Values:
❤️ Compassion and dignity • 🌱 Resilience • ⚖️ Integrity & equity • 🔍 Accountability • 🤝 Collaboration
Collaboration Frameworks

How Institutions Partner With Us

Bilateral Grants & Programs

Partner with THECCODA on multi-year institutional programs for safe haven operations, vulnerable persons care, and psychosocial disaster resilience in Mount Elgon communities.

Care Haven & School Bursaries

Sponsor classrooms, scholastic bursary blocks for 100+ vulnerable orphans, or contribute clinical trauma recovery equipment with full transparency and verified audit reporting.

VSLA & Livelihood Seed Capital

Provide seed matching funds for our 84+ community-managed Village Savings and Loan Associations empowering widows, single mothers, and youth to launch self-sustaining micro-enterprises.
