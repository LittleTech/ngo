Zero-Tolerance Child Protection

Child Safeguarding & Whistleblower Portal

Our safeguarding protocols emanate directly from our core values of Compassion and dignity, Integrity & equity, and Accountability, protecting vulnerable individuals as we advance our mission to bring people together to build hope, peace and community and our vision of a world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

Child Protection Guided by Our 5 Core Values:
❤️ Compassion and dignity • 🌱 Resilience • ⚖️ Integrity & equity • 🔍 Accountability • 🤝 Collaboration

Our Inviolable Child Protection Code

Every counselor, teacher, haven caregiver, volunteer, and driver at THECCODA undergoes mandatory background vetting, signs our legally binding Child Safeguarding Charter, and is trained in trauma-informed safeguarding. If you suspect or witness any vulnerable child or person in distress, report immediately below or via our toll-free hotline.

Download Safeguarding Charter (PDF) Confidential Escalation Desk: safeguarding@theccoda.org
