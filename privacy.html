Privacy Policy & Vulnerable Persons Data Protection

Last updated: March 2026 • Makalama Headquarters, Bududa District

Trauma Healing Childcare and Development Association (THECCODA), established in 1997 and registered under Uganda NGO Board Reg. #INBR199708, is guided by our vision of "A world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services" and mission to "Bring people together to build hope, peace and community". Grounded in our core values of Compassion and dignity, Resilience, Integrity & equity, Accountability, and Collaboration, we uphold the highest standards of confidentiality, ethical stewardship, and child protection under Ugandan and international law.

1. Protection of Vulnerable Minors and Case Files

All counseling records, trauma case files, haven intake registers, and beneficiary identities are strictly protected. Beneficiary stories and photos published on our website are used solely with informed, written consent of legal guardians or caregivers, with names altered where necessary to preserve personal privacy and safety.

2. Information We Collect from Supporters & Donors

When you donate via Mobile Money, bank transfer, or card, or submit volunteer and partnership forms, we collect voluntary contact information including your name, email, phone number, and transaction receipt data. Financial card payments are handled securely through certified PCI-compliant gateways; THECCODA does not store card numbers or banking PINs.

3. Whistleblower & Safeguarding Submissions

Reports submitted through our safeguarding desk can be transmitted anonymously. All submissions are treated with strict confidentiality by our Designated Safeguarding Lead and Child Welfare Committee.

4. Donor Privacy & Data Rights

Supporters may elect to make anonymous gifts. We never sell, lease, or distribute donor details to third-party marketing companies. For data inquiries or record updates, contact our Secretariat at privacy@theccoda.org.
