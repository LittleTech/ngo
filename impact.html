Our 29-Year Humanitarian Impact Record

Our 29-year impact record is the living proof of our mission to bring people together to build hope, peace and community, relentlessly advancing our vision of a world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services.

Verified Milestones

Measurable Results Across 29 Years (1997 – 2026)

Field casework records, clinical psychology reports, and statutory annual returns submitted to the Uganda NGO Board.

38,500+

Vulnerable Persons Cared For

Direct relief, emergency food rations, home visits, and assistive care provided to orphans, neglected elders, and persons with disabilities.

19,200+

Trauma Counseling Sessions

Individual psychotherapy, non-verbal child play therapy, grief counseling, and family reconciliation circles delivered by certified counselors.

3,400+

Safe Haven Sanctuary Beds

Children and vulnerable persons rescued from acute crisis, abandonment, or disasters and accommodated in our Makalama Haven.

7,800+

Education Bursary Scholars

Orphans and vulnerable students sponsored through primary and secondary education across 48 partner schools, with zero dropout rates.

84+

Caregiver VSLAs Active

Community savings cooperatives mobilizing local capital for over 1,500 widowed mothers and grandmothers, boosting household food security.

95%

Trauma Remission Rate

Documented alleviation of severe PTSD, panic symptoms, and mutism in children after 8 to 12 weeks of clinical expressive play therapy.

Guiding Commitments

How Our 5 Core Values Shape Our Results

Every outcome we record is anchored in our institutional ethical code.

❤️

1. Compassion and dignity

Ensuring 38,500+ vulnerable individuals receive assistance that restores human pride and worth without condescension.

🌱

2. Resilience

Building durable inner coping skills (95% trauma remission) and economic self-sufficiency (84+ caregiver VSLAs).

⚖️

3. Integrity & equity

Distributing bursaries and emergency relief transparently based strictly on vulnerability without favoritism.

🔍

4. Accountability

29 consecutive years of statutory reporting, verified receipts, and open external audit statements for public inspection.

🤝

5. Collaboration

Partnering deeply with Bududa District Health Teams, 48 partner schools, and faith networks for compound impact.
