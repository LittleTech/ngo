Terms of Service & Organizational Governance

Last updated: March 2026 • Makalama Village, Bukibino Sub-County, Bududa District

By accessing and utilizing the website, digital resources, and donation systems of Trauma Healing Childcare and Development Association (THECCODA), you agree to comply with and be bound by the following governance terms.

1. Organization Mission, Vision & Core Values

THECCODA is a registered national and local NGO (Uganda NGO Board Reg. #INBR199708) established in 1997 in Makalama Village, Bukibino Sub-County, Manjiya County, Bududa District, Uganda. Our slogan is "Building a community driven with faith and hope". Our vision is "A world enabling vulnerable individuals have access to better social services". Our mission is to "Bring people together to build hope, peace and community". All operations, staff conduct, and digital platforms are governed by our five core values: (1) Compassion and dignity, (2) Resilience, (3) Integrity & equity, (4) Accountability, and (5) Collaboration.

2. Dignity & Child Imagery Safeguarding

Photographs and beneficiary testimonies on this website are presented strictly for genuine advocacy and fundraising with appropriate consents. Any unauthorized reproduction, predatory usage, or commercial re-distribution of child or vulnerable persons imagery is strictly forbidden under the Uganda Children Act and international law.

3. Donations, Mobile Money & Bank Transfer Stewardship

Contributions made through MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Centenary Bank, or international card gateways go directly toward verified humanitarian programs, haven care, bursaries, and community healing. THECCODA issues verifiable official digital receipts for every recorded donation.

4. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Official inquiries should be addressed to info@theccoda.org or the Secretariat at Makalama Headquarters, Bududa.
